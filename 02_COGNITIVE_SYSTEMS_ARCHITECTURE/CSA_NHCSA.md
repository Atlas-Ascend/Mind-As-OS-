# CSA / NHCSA

Cognitive Systems Architecture is the structural specification layer between theory and runtime.

Minimum architectural concerns:
- perception and salience
- attention allocation
- affect/state signals
- working and durable memory interfaces
- symbolic/semantic interpretation
- self/identity models
- relational/world models
- state orientation
- deliberation and planning
- metacognitive observability
- action authority boundaries
- feedback and learning
- coherence and recovery
- human-machine handoff

`NHCSA` is retained as a historical naming variant/candidate until an exact source contract settles its relation to CSA/CSA-95.
