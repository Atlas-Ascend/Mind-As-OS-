# AURA / SAGE / NAVI

## AURA — inner telemetry
Question: **What is happening in my current state and pattern?**
Owns reflection/state-recognition telemetry. Read/advisory only.

## SAGE — relational telemetry
Loop: **See → Assess → Gauge → Engage**. Engage may validly resolve to non-engagement. Distinguishes self-state from claims about another person or environment.

## NAVI — navigation / interpretation
Places observations and interpretations into a navigable context, routes ambiguity and governance concerns, and helps orient the system without becoming the final truth or execution authority.

These three are complementary, not aliases: self telemetry, relational telemetry, orientation/interpretation.
