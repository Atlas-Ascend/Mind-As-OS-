# NHCM

Current GARI portfolio preserves **NHCM / hierarchical cognition mapping** inside Cognition Architecture. A current estate governance adapter describes NHCM as **cognitive ontology / classification service**.

Mind As OS therefore treats NHCM as the classification/ontology bridge between research models and typed cognitive representations.

NHCM may classify observations, states, relations, hypotheses, patterns and cognitive objects. Classification does not imply diagnosis, truth certification or software authority.

Historical NHCM paper/trilogy lineages remain research artifacts and should be linked through GARI-Provenance rather than rewritten here.
