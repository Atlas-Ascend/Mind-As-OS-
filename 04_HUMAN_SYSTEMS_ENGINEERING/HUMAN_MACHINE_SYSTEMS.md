# Human–Machine Systems Engineering

The operator and estate form one sociotechnical control system.

Human-side state may shape intent/context. Machine-side execution remains capability-governed.

Engineering questions include:
- can the operator identify current owner/state?
- can AI recommendation be distinguished from verified fact?
- can automation failure be detected?
- can the operator interrupt/override safely?
- is workload observable?
- are confidence and provenance visible?
- can the system degrade safely when human attention is unavailable?
- are write/destructive actions separately authorized?

The design objective is calibrated collaboration, not removal of human sovereignty.
