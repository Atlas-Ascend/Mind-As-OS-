# Identity, Self-Governance and Meaning Architecture

Separate four questions:
1. **Epistemic:** what is supported by evidence?
2. **Interpretive:** what meanings are possible?
3. **Governance:** what response is responsible/aligned?
4. **Authorization:** what action is actually permitted?

Identity state is an observable/modelable context, not a totalizing label. Meaning can guide priorities without becoming factual proof. Stewardship governs care, consent, boundaries, reversibility, consequence and non-domination.
